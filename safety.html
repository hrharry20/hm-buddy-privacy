Safety standards

HM Buddy · Harbansh Singh [H S Software Solutions] · Updated 20 October 2026

Protection of children

HM Buddy prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion, trafficking and all child sexual abuse material. These prohibitions apply to every user, account, conversation and media transfer.

Adults-only audience

HM Buddy is for people aged 18 or older. Child registration and parent-managed accounts are not offered. Contact support if a child has registered or if you suspect exploitation or other unsafe activity. Never share passwords or contact details without consent.

Reporting and blocking

Long-press a message and choose Report to submit details and selected message evidence to support. You can block users from conversation details. Use Account deletion / Support or hssoftwaresolutions2026@gmail.com for a safety concern. Never send illegal images or passwords by email. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Operator response

The designated safety contact is Harbansh Singh at hssoftwaresolutions2026@gmail.com. The operator must review reports, restrict access or remove content when appropriate, and report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the relevant authority as required by law. This is a human review process, not an automated content-detection or emergency response service. The operator must maintain an appropriate reporting and preservation procedure before public launch.

Privacy and deletion

You may request account/data deletion through Account deletion. Read the privacy policy for data use, server access and retention.
