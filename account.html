Account deletion and support

HM Buddy · Harbansh Singh [H S Software Solutions]

Request account deletion

To request permanent deletion of your HM Buddy account and associated data, email hssoftwaresolutions2026@gmail.com with the subject “HM Buddy account deletion”. Include your registered mobile number with country code and your account name. Do not include your password or identity documents.

Support will verify ownership before deleting the account. If ownership cannot be verified from your initial request, support will explain the next verification step. This request can be made even if the app is uninstalled.

Deletion removes the profile, password hash, sessions, messages authored by the account, owned server uploads, latest shared location and conversation memberships from the active database. Files saved on your own or recipients' devices, other people's messages and screenshots are not removed. Hosting-provider logs and backups follow the provider's retention settings.

You may also use the password-confirmed deletion page in app version 0.1.8 or later: Open account deletion. If this page is not yet available for your pilot version, use the email request above.

Privacy, safety or account help

Contact hssoftwaresolutions2026@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, identity documents or illegal images. HM Buddy is for adults aged 18 or older. If a child has registered, ask support to review and remove that account.

Selected-message safety reporting is available in app version 0.1.8 or later; earlier pilot users can use this support address. Immediate danger requires local emergency services.
