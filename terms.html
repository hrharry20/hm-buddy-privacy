Adult accounts

HM Buddy is for adults aged 18 or older. Register with your own number and a strong password. Do not create an account for a child, impersonate another person, use another person's number without permission, or share your password.

Consent and respectful use

By registering or accepting an updated policy you agree to these rules and the privacy policy. Share only content you have the right and permission to share. Contact only people who agree to communicate with you. Respect blocks and other people's privacy.

Harassment, threats, bullying, stalking, impersonation, fraud, unlawful content, hate, sexual exploitation, illegal sexual content, non-consensual intimate imagery, grooming, sextortion and child sexual abuse or exploitation are prohibited. Do not create, request, share or distribute child sexual abuse material. Do not use the service to solicit personal information from children or bypass a parent's controls.

Safety and enforcement

Use Report on a message, block the person, or contact support. Anyone can contact support by email about a safety concern. Support may restrict an account or remove content after reviewing a report. See the safety standards. Do not forward illegal media to support. Immediate danger requires local emergency services.

Service limits

This is an independently developed pilot with no guarantee of uninterrupted availability or complete WhatsApp feature parity. Live files require both original devices online. The app has no subscription, but your internet connection and hosting-provider limits still apply. Calls request HD where supported; actual quality varies. Large calls display limited concurrent tracks. This build is not end-to-end encrypted and must not be used as an emergency service.

Leaving the service

You may delete your account. Recipient device copies are outside our control. Contact support for assistance or questions.
