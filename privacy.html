Privacy policy

HM Buddy · Harbansh Singh [H S Software Solutions] · Updated 20 October 2026

Contact: hssoftwaresolutions2026@gmail.com. HM Buddy is an independent messenger. It does not connect to WhatsApp. This policy describes the Cloudflare-hosted HM Buddy service.

Information used

  • Account name, self-declared mobile number, password hash, profile description/photo, sessions and linked-device requests.
  • Conversation messages, participants, delivery/read information, call membership and media-session metadata.
  • With adult opt-in, phone numbers from the address book are sent over HTTPS to find registered contacts. The app retains matched contacts locally. Submitted address-book batches are not stored as an address-book database.
  • Photos, video, documents and voice notes you choose to share. New live-file bytes are relayed through Cloudflare and saved on devices, not in persistent cloud file storage. Profile images and earlier stored attachments may be stored on the server.
  • Camera and microphone access when you record or join a call. Optional live location sends your latest precise coordinates and accuracy to the server and approved conversation members. A map sends coordinates to OpenStreetMap when opened.
  • Security rate-limit information, including hashed network addresses for some authentication guards, identifiers and timestamps. Cloudflare can also process network/device metadata under its own service terms.
  • Adult-account policy consent and timestamps. Safety reports contain the details and selected message evidence the reporter chooses to submit.

Purpose and disclosure

Data is used to operate accounts, discover contacts, deliver conversations, transfer media, connect calls, prevent misuse and review safety concerns. There are no app ads, advertising SDKs, data sales or paid app subscriptions in this build.

Names, self-declared numbers and profiles are visible to registered members of this service. Only conversation/call participants can access those conversations/calls. Support can review reports submitted to it, and the service operator can access server data.

Cloudflare provides hosting, database and call/transfer relay services. OpenStreetMap provides map displays when requested. Data may be processed where providers operate. Sharing with intended recipients and processing by these service providers are necessary to provide the selected functions. We may disclose data where legally required.

Adults only

This release is intended only for people aged 18 or older. Registration requires adult confirmation and policy acceptance. It does not offer child accounts or parent-managed registration. Do not create an account for a child. We do not collect birth dates or verify age using identity documents. If a child has registered, contact support to request review and removal of the account and associated data.

Storage and retention

Account and conversation data generally stays until the account or relevant data is deleted. Status updates expire after 24 hours and scheduled cleanup removes them. Live-file offers expire after seven days; offer metadata can remain with messages. Only the latest shared location is stored and is cleared on stop, expiry, sign-out of the sharing session. Sessions expire. Security rate-limit rows are normally cleared after 48 hours. Safety reports are removed after 30 days by scheduled cleanup unless removed earlier with an associated account. The operator must handle legally required reporting or preservation separately.

Active server deletion does not erase files saved by recipients, messages authored by other people, screenshots or data independently retained by other people. Provider logs and backups follow provider retention settings and may not disappear at the same instant as active database records. Contact support about data-access or deletion questions.

Account deletion and choices

Delete account and associated data

This web resource works without reinstalling the app. Confirm using your mobile number and password. If you cannot authenticate, contact support for identity verification and assistance; do not send your password by email. You can deny/revoke device permissions, pause background alerts, stop location sharing and block contacts. Saved device files must be removed separately from Gallery, Music or Downloads.

Security and limits

HTTPS protects app-to-server transport. Native and web session storage use local encryption. Messages and media are not end-to-end encrypted; the operator and hosting service can access server-side data. Mobile numbers are self-declared and are not verified through SMS. Check the identity of your contacts personally. Do not share passwords or rely on this pilot for emergency communications. Cloudflare free quotas, device availability and connectivity can limit operation.

Changes and questions

Material policy changes may require a new policy acceptance before account changes or communication can continue. Contact hssoftwaresolutions2026@gmail.com for privacy questions, safety reports or account help.
